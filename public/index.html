How to unblock your microphone:

  1. Click the lock icon (🔒) or site settings icon on the left side of your browser address bar (next to localhost:3000).
  2. Find Microphone and change it from Blocked to Allow.
  3. Click "Try Microphone Again" below!

💡 Windows Check: Make sure Windows Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → "Let desktop apps access your microphone" is turned ON.
